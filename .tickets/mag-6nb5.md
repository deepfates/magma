# Compose a semantic local audio mixer and shared soundboard

Unify video, radio, ambience, voice, ritual cues, and playful pads so shared triggers and local listening consent do not fight over the device.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each source has understandable gain/mute behavior and master sensory controls always win.
- Built-in start, finish, breathe, reset, tiny-win, encouragement, and playful pads trigger a brief identified room effect.
- Audio plays only for people who opted in; visual feedback still converges.
- Favorite pads and labels can be arranged and saved.
- Cooldowns prevent spam without introducing role UI.
