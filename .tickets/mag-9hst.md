# Make Change the view a queue, scene shelf, and save surface

Bring the existing media queue into the current one-screen Porch as contextual Window, Radio, and World lanes with now/next controls and named scene cards.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Anyone can add, reorder, choose now, choose next, and remove non-active entries.
- The current composition is readable in one line and Treasure Island remains the default.
- Named scenes can be saved, restored, and deleted by anyone.
- The shelf works on a 390px viewport without horizontal document overflow.
